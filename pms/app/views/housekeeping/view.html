Room cleaning

Which rooms housekeeping should do first, from the day's check-ins and check-outs (03:00 to 03:00, like the Check-ins page). Each room counts its own check-out, so delayed groups are included.

Priority list

1 Urgent a group leaves and another arrives the same day — clean in the gap, tightest first 2 Check Required a group arrives into a room nobody leaves today — ready before arrival 3 Checkout House keeping a group leaves, no one arrives today — clean after they leave 4 Daily Housekeeping guests stay on — daily service

“Done” ticks are kept on this device for the day.
